# Research Output: Latest AI-Fusion Papers (2025-2026)

## 1. Nature / Nature-Family Journals

- Wang AM, Rea C, So O, Dawson C, Garnier DT et al. "Active ramp-down control and trajectory design for tokamaks with neural differential equations and reinforcement learning." *Communications Physics* (Nature), 2025. DOI: 10.1038/s42005-025-02146-6. Neural ODEs + RL for safe tokamak ramp-down trajectories.

- Jalalvand A, Kim SK, Seo J et al. "Multimodal super-resolution: discovering hidden physics and its application to fusion plasmas." *Nature Communications*, 2025. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-63492-1. First multimodal super-resolution framework for fusion diagnostics.

## 2. Nuclear Fusion

- Subbotin GF, Sorokin DI, Nurgaliev MR et al. "Demonstration of reconstruction-free static magnetic control of DIII-D plasma with deep reinforcement learning." *Nuclear Fusion*, 2026. DOI: 10.1088/1741-4326/ae34c6. DRL controls plasma without equilibrium reconstruction.

## 3. Physics of Plasmas

- Rea C & Benjamin S. "A review of machine learning-driven studies of tearing modes in tokamaks." *Physics of Plasmas* 33(5), 050501, 2026. Comprehensive review of ML for tearing mode prediction/control.

- Schissel DP, Nazikian RM & Gibbs T. "Summary report from the mini-conference on digital twins for fusion research." *Physics of Plasmas*, 2025. Community roadmap for digital twin development.

## 4. Foundation Models / LLMs

- Boschi T, Loreti A et al. "TokaMind: A Multi-Modal Transformer Foundation Model for Tokamak Plasma Dynamics." arXiv:2602.15084, 2026. First dedicated foundation model for fusion plasmas.

- Almeldein A et al. "Exploring the capabilities of the frontier large language models for nuclear energy research." arXiv:2506.19863, 2025. Evaluates LLMs for nuclear/fusion tasks.

- Gorse V, Mitteau R, Marot J. "Decision support for in-operation monitoring of the WEST tokamak first wall using multimodal LLM on infrared imaging." *Knowledge-Based Systems*, 2025. Multimodal LLM for real-time PFC monitoring at WEST.

## 5. Digital Twins + RL

- Bhatia N, Costa R, Pamela SJP et al. "Visualizing digital twins of fusion power plants using NVIDIA Omniverse." *AIP Advances* 15(4), 045018, 2025. Immersive 3D digital twin visualization.

- Meng L. "AI as a Foundational Enabler for Magnetic Confinement Fusion: From Plasma Control to Autonomous Reactor Digital Twins." SSRN 5897315, 2025. Comprehensive AI architecture for autonomous reactor operation.

## 6. Generative AI for Fusion

- Padidar M et al. "Diffusion for Fusion: Designing Stellarators with Generative AI." arXiv:2511.20445, 2025. Diffusion models for stellarator design.

- Chen N et al. "Regulation Compliant AI for Fusion: Real-Time Image Analysis-Based Control of Divertor Detachment in Tokamaks." arXiv:2507.02897, 2025. Regulation-compliant AI for divertor control.

## 7. Multi-Modal Diagnostics

- Kaldas S et al. "Developing Autoencoder Models for Multi-Diagnostic Electron Density Processing in Tokamaks within a SciML Framework." *Journal of Fusion Energy*, 2026. DOI: 10.1007/s10894-026-00568-w. SciML autoencoders for multi-diagnostic fusion.
