# Research Output: AI Safety, V&V, and Regulatory Frameworks for Fusion (2024-2026)

## 1. Explainable AI (XAI) for Fusion

- Bonalumi et al. (2024). "eXplainable artificial intelligence applied to algorithms for disruption prediction in tokamak devices." *Frontiers in Physics*. DOI: 10.3389/fphy.2024.1359656. Occlusion/saliency maps for CNN disruption predictor.

- Chen et al. (2026). "Regulation compliant AI for fusion: explainable image-based feedback control of divertor detachment in DIII-D tokamak." 3 citations. Regulatory compliance + explainability for real-time control.

- Pyragius et al. (2024). "Application of interpretable machine learning for cross-diagnostic inference on the ST40 spherical tokamak." 3 citations.

## 2. V&V and Trustworthiness

- Tian et al. (2025). "Progress in Verification Research of AI-Integrated Software for Nuclear Energy Based on Industrial Big Data." IEEE.

- Pelekis et al. (2024). "Trustworthy artificial intelligence in the energy sector: Landscape analysis and evaluation framework." IEEE. Sector-wide trustworthiness evaluation.

- Silva et al. (2024). "Safety in Artificial Intelligence: Challenges and Opportunities for the US National Labs and Beyond." OSTI/US DOE.

## 3. Certification and Regulation

- Cha (2024). "Towards an international regulatory framework for AI safety: lessons from the IAEA's nuclear safety regulations." Maps IAEA nuclear safety to AI governance.

- Klebanov & Lizikova (2025). "Artificial Intelligence in Nuclear Energy: Legal Challenges and International Cooperation."

- Cancila et al. (2024). "Research Directions on AI and Nuclear." *EPJ Web of Conferences*. Multi-stakeholder roadmap.

## 4. Uncertainty Quantification

- Amnell, Acerbi & Jarvinen (2025). "Bayesian likelihood-free inference for effective uncertainty quantification in plasma physics simulators."

- Abbate et al. (2024). "Combing physics-based and data-driven predictions for quantitatively accurate models that extrapolate well; with application to DIII-D, AUG, and ITER." Hybrid physics-ML with implicit UQ.

- Mehta et al. (2024). "Automated experimental design of safe rampdowns via probabilistic machine learning." TCV safe rampdown design.

## 5. Human-in-the-Loop and Fail-Safe

- Gutierrez et al. (2026). "Human-in-the-Loop Meta Bayesian Optimization for Fusion Energy and Scientific Applications." arXiv:2605.00068.

- Jiang et al. (2025). "Human-Machine-Environment Engineering Framework for Nuclear Safety Management of Chinese Future Fusion Reactor." CFETR safety framework.

- Benito & Barrientos (2024). "An Intelligent Human-Machine Interface Architecture for Long-Term Remote Robot Handling in Fusion Reactor Environments."

## 6. Adversarial Robustness

- Roy et al. (2026). "Adversarial Vulnerabilities in Neural Operator Digital Twins: Gradient-Free Attacks on Nuclear Thermal-Hydraulic Surrogates." Adversarial attack surfaces in neural operator surrogates.

- Chayapathy et al. (2025). "Time Series Augmentations with Unsupervised Viewmakers for Robust Disruption Prediction in Nuclear Fusion." Data augmentation for robust disruption prediction.

## 7. Digital Twins and Holistic AI-for-Fusion

- Battye & Perinpanayagam (2025). "Digital twins in fusion energy research: Current state and future directions." 19 citations. Comprehensive survey.

- Agnello et al. (2026). "Challenges and opportunities for AI to help deliver fusion energy." arXiv:2603.25777. UKAEA/STFC multi-stakeholder roadmap.

## Key Gap

Formal verification of neural networks specifically for fusion control remains sparse. The field is shifting from "can ML work for fusion?" to "can we trust and certify ML for fusion?"
