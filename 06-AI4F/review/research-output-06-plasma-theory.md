# Research Output: AI for Plasma Theory and Fundamental Physics (2024-2026)

## 1. ML for Turbulence Theory and Transport Model Discovery

- Clavier B, Zarzoso D, Del-Castillo-Negrete D, Frenod E. "Generative-machine-learning surrogate model of plasma turbulence." *Phys Rev E* 111, L013202 (2025). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevE.111.L013202. Uses generative ML to build surrogate models of plasma turbulence, explicitly capturing zonal flow dynamics.

- Gahr C, Farcas IG, Jenko F. "Scientific machine learning based reduced-order models for plasma turbulence simulations." *Phys Plasmas* 31, 113904 (2024). Develops SciML-based ROMs for gyrokinetic turbulence, enabling fast parameter scans intractable with full simulations.

## 2. Neural ODEs for Plasma Transport

- Liu Z, Stacey WM. "Application of Neural ODEs for ITER burning plasma dynamics." arXiv:2408.14404 (2024). Extends Neural ODEs to model core-edge-SOL coupled transport in ITER-relevant scenarios with neutral beam injection sources.

- Zhu B, Wang H, Wu J, Ren H. "Prediction of mode structure using a novel physics-embedded Neural ODE method." *Plasma Phys Controlled Fusion* (2025). DOI: 10.1088/1361-6587/adfd13. Introduces an exposed-latent-state Neural ODE for MHD mode structure prediction.

## 3. Symbolic Regression for Discovering Plasma Equations

- Faraji F, Reza M, Knoll A. "Discovery of discretized differential equations from data: benchmarking and application to a plasma system." *J Appl Phys* (2025). Applies symbolic regression to discover governing equations of plasma systems from simulation data.

- Burles S, Camporeale E. "The machine learning approach to moment closure relations for plasma: a review." arXiv:2511.22486 (2025). Reviews ML (including symbolic regression) for discovering closure relations in Vlasov-based plasma models.

## 4. Physics-Informed Neural Operators for Fusion PDEs

- Carey N, Zanisi L, Pamela S, Gopakumar V et al. "Neural operator surrogate models of plasma edge simulations: feasibility and data efficiency." *Nucl Fusion* (2025). Demonstrates neural operator surrogates for edge plasma simulations with favorable data efficiency.

- Rahman MM, Bai Z, King JR et al. "Sparsified time-dependent Fourier neural operators for fusion simulations." *Phys Plasmas* 31, 123902 (2024). Develops sparse FNO architectures tailored for fusion-relevant time-dependent PDEs.

## 5. AI-Assisted Theory Discovery

- Joglekar AS, Thomas AGR et al. "Differentiable programming for plasma physics: from diagnostics to discovery and design." arXiv:2603.11231 (2026). Proposes differentiable programming as a paradigm for automated physics discovery in plasmas.

## 6. ML for MHD Stability Analysis

- Rutigliano N, Murari A, Gaudio P et al. "Optimisation of PINN architecture for tokamak equilibrium reconstruction." *Plasma Phys Controlled Fusion* (2026). DOI: 10.1088/1361-6587/ae54c9. Optimizes PINN architectures for accurate MHD equilibrium reconstruction.

- Thun T, Merlo A, Conlin R et al. "Improving ideal MHD equilibrium accuracy with PINNs." *Nucl Fusion* (2026). DOI: 10.1088/1741-4326/ae2937.

## 7. Neural Network Approaches to Kinetic Theory

- Ilin V, Hu J. "A neural score-based particle method for the Vlasov-Maxwell-Landau system." arXiv:2603.25832 (2026). Trains score networks on-the-fly for kinetic plasma simulation while preserving momentum and energy conservation.

- Powis AT, Corona Rivera D, Khrabry A. "Accelerating kinetic plasma simulations with ML-generated initial conditions." *Phys Plasmas* (2026). Uses CNNs to generate initial conditions for Vlasov-Poisson/Maxwell simulations.

## 8. ML for Wave-Plasma Interactions

- Sieben M, Weiland M, Bilato R et al. "Surrogate modeling of combined full-wave code TORIC and Fokker-Planck solver SSFPQL for ICRH minority heating." *Nucl Fusion* (2026). DOI: 10.1088/1741-4326/ae3d4b. Neural network surrogates for coupled ICRH wave-heating and Fokker-Planck solvers at ASDEX Upgrade.

## 9. Turbulence-Zonal Flow Interactions

- Li H, Wang L, Fu YL et al. "Surrogate model of turbulent transport in fusion plasmas using machine learning." *Nucl Fusion* (2025). DOI: 10.1088/1741-4326/ad8b5b. ML surrogate capturing zonal-flow-mediated turbulent transport.

## 10. Data-Driven Reduced Models

- Faraji F, Reza M, Knoll A, Kutz JN. "Data-driven local operator finding for reduced-order modeling of plasma systems." *J Phys D: Appl Phys* (2025). DOI: 10.1088/1361-6463/adbe85. Discovers local operators for ROMs of plasma dynamics.

- Solheim A, Lim K, Deparis S, Ricci P. "Data-driven model order reduction for accelerating boundary plasma turbulence simulations." *J Plasma Phys* (2026). Targets ITER/DEMO-scale edge turbulence acceleration.

- Garrido Gonzalez D, Saura N, Beyer P et al. "An AI-driven reduced order model for edge tokamak turbulence." *Phys Plasmas* 32, 092301 (2025). Combines physics-based modes with data-driven nonlinear coupling for edge turbulence ROMs.
