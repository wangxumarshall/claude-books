## 3. What Makes This Paper Publishable

1. **Honest self-assessment**: The paper prominently acknowledges that 81% of sources are preprints and that experimental validation is concentrated on DIII-D (67%). This transparency is rare and commendable.

2. **Evidence quality framework**: The Level A-D stratification system is a genuine methodological contribution that other review papers should adopt.

3. **Comprehensive coverage**: 146 references across 8 core dimensions + 6 extension topics, with Mermaid-rendered figures.

4. **Critical assessment**: The paper does not merely survey — it critically evaluates maturity (TRL framework), identifies failure modes (7 categories), and distinguishes demos from deployment (Section 10.11).

5. **Regulatory awareness**: The addition of regulatory pathway discussion (NRC, IAEA, EU AI Act) and formal verification references (Lyapunov barriers for DRL) shows awareness of the safety certification landscape.

6. **Forward-looking**: Coverage of AI agents, MCP, PDE foundation models, and multi-agent scientific discovery systems positions the paper at the frontier of the field.
