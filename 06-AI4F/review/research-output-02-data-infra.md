# Research Output: Data Infrastructure + Open Science for AI-Fusion (2024-2026)

## 1. ITER IMAS

- Pankin AY et al. "NIMROD-to-IMAS workflow for extended-magnetohydrodynamic data with reusable datasets and implications for IMAS schema development." arXiv:2605.23121 (May 2026). Workflow converting NIMROD MHD data into IMAS Data Dictionary v4 records with COCOS-consistent coordinates and provenance metadata.

## 2. IAEA Fusion Data Lake

- Gahle DS and Barbarino M. "The IAEA Fusion Data Lake Project -- Accelerating AI and Big Data Applications through Open Science and FAIR Data." arXiv:2604.01797 (Apr 2026). 24 institutions across 11 countries. Three components: international data catalogue, centralized storage, data federation. FAIR-compliant AI training datasets.

## 3. Open-Source Tools

- **TORAX**: Citrin J et al. "TORAX: A Fast and Differentiable Tokamak Transport Simulator in JAX." arXiv:2406.06718 (Jun 2024). Google DeepMind's open-source differentiable 1D core transport simulator in JAX.

- **Gym-TORAX**: Mouchamps A et al. "Gym-TORAX: Open-source software for integrating reinforcement learning with plasma control simulators." arXiv:2510.11283 (Oct 2025). OpenAI Gym-compatible RL environment wrapping TORAX.

- **DESC**: Panici D et al. "DESC Part I-III." arXiv (2022). Differentiable stellarator/tokamak equilibrium code in JAX.

- **FreeGS**: Referenced in Yuksek N and Golfinopoulos T. arXiv:2603.01208 (Mar 2026). Free-boundary equilibrium solver.

## 4. Multi-Machine Databases

- Maris A et al. "Correlation of the L-mode density limit with edge collisionality." arXiv:2406.18442 (Jun 2024). Multi-machine database (AUG, C-Mod, DIII-D, TCV) for cross-device benchmarking.

## 5. FRNN

- Kates-Harbeck J et al. Nature 568, 526-531 (2019). Foundational FRNN work. No dedicated 2024-2026 FRNN publications found — framework extensions documented in Rea et al. RMP 2024.

## 6. OMFIT

- Lyons BC et al. "Flexible, integrated modeling of tokamak stability, transport, equilibrium, and pedestal physics." arXiv:2305.09683 (2023). OMFIT unified workflow with Bayesian inference and UQ.

## Key Gap

EUROfusion IDP framework lacks dedicated 2024-2026 publications. Documentation available through EUROfusion technical reports and gateway.euro-fusion.org.
