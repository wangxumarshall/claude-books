# PSI Research And Design Plan

## Goal

Deeply understand all PSI research articles in this directory, with extra emphasis on `04-*` and `05-*`, then produce a fair, evidence-based PSI architecture design and implementation plan plus an adversarial review.

## Phases

| Phase | Status | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Inventory and planning | complete | Identify source articles and establish persistent research notes. |
| 2. Article synthesis | complete | Extract claims, evidence, design implications, and tensions from every article. |
| 3. Fair necessity/value assessment | complete | Evaluate PSI's necessity and value without assuming a favorable conclusion. |
| 4. Formal architecture and implementation plan | complete | Draft the PSI proposal covering philosophy, essence, principles, scope, execution, expected effects, and roadmap. |
| 5. Adversarial review and revision | complete | Attack the proposal from technical, product, organizational, and risk angles; revise into a higher-value final version. |

## Working Rules

- Treat source articles as primary local evidence.
- Separate source claims from independent judgment.
- Preserve both pro-PSI and anti-PSI arguments.
- Emphasize feasibility, interfaces, migration path, and measurable outcomes.
